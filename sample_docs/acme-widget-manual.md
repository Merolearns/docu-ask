# Acme Widget 3000 — User Manual (SAMPLE DOCUMENT)

This is a fictional product manual written as sample data for testing. Any
resemblance to real products is coincidental.

## Getting started

Unbox the Acme Widget 3000 and place it on a flat surface near a power outlet.
Connect the included power adapter to the port on the back of the unit, then
press and hold the power button for three seconds. The status LED will blink
blue while the widget boots, which takes about forty seconds. When the LED
turns solid green, the widget is ready to use.

To pair the widget with your phone, open the Acme companion app and tap "Add
device". The app will discover the widget over Bluetooth automatically. If
pairing fails, move the phone closer and make sure no other Bluetooth device
is connected to the widget.

## Factory reset

If the widget behaves erratically, a factory reset usually fixes it. To reset
the Acme Widget 3000, press and hold the power button and the volume-down
button together for ten seconds until the LED flashes red three times. Release
both buttons. The widget will reboot and all settings, paired devices, and
stored schedules will be erased. You will need to run the initial setup again
after a factory reset.

## Battery and charging

The widget has a 5000 mAh rechargeable battery. A full charge takes roughly
two hours with the included adapter and lasts up to eighteen hours of typical
use. The LED turns amber when the battery drops below twenty percent. Do not
use third-party fast chargers rated above 25 watts, as they can damage the
charging circuit and void the warranty.

## Troubleshooting

Widget won't turn on: check that the adapter is firmly seated and try a
different outlet. App can't find the widget: restart Bluetooth on your phone
and power-cycle the widget. LED stuck on blinking blue: the firmware update
was interrupted — leave the widget plugged in for an hour and it will resume.

## Warranty

The Acme Widget 3000 carries a one-year limited warranty covering defects in
materials and workmanship. The warranty does not cover water damage, drops,
or use of unauthorized chargers.
