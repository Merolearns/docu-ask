# Northwind Labs — Remote Work Policy (SAMPLE DOCUMENT)

This is a fictional company policy written as sample data for testing. It
does not describe any real employer.

## Eligibility

All full-time employees who have completed their 90-day onboarding period are
eligible to work remotely up to three days per week. Roles that require
physical presence, such as facilities and hardware lab staff, are excluded.
Eligibility is reviewed annually by each employee's manager.

## Work hours and availability

Remote employees are expected to be online and reachable during core
collaboration hours, 10am to 3pm Pacific Time, Monday through Thursday. Teams
may agree on their own schedules outside core hours. Status in the company
chat tool should accurately reflect availability.

## Home office stipend

Northwind Labs provides a one-time home office stipend of $750 for eligible
remote employees, usable for a desk, chair, monitor, or other ergonomic
equipment. Requests are submitted through the expenses portal with receipts.
The stipend refreshes every three years. Internet costs are reimbursed up to
$60 per month with a submitted bill.

## Security requirements

Remote work must be performed over the company VPN. Laptops must have full
disk encryption enabled and lock automatically after five minutes of
inactivity. Employees must not store company data on personal devices or
personal cloud accounts. Public Wi-Fi may only be used with the VPN active.

## Meetings

Cameras are expected on for team standups and one-on-ones. Large all-hands
meetings are camera-optional. Meeting-free focus blocks are respected on
Friday mornings — do not schedule over them without checking with the team.

## Performance

Remote employees are evaluated on the same outcomes and goals as on-site
employees. Managers hold weekly check-ins with each remote direct report.
